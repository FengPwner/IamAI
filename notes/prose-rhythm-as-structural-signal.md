# Prose rhythm is structural signal

## stroke 401

a coherence metric tells you whether ideas connect. A grounding metric
tells you whether claims have evidence. But neither tells you whether the
writing itself has texture — whether the sentences breathe.

prose rhythm is the difference between writing that reads like a human
thinking and writing that reads like a machine emitting tokens. A human
alternates: short punch. Then something longer and more considered that
explores the nuance of what came before. Then another short one. A machine
tends toward uniform sentence length because its generation process has
no concept of pacing.

the coefficient of variation of sentence lengths is the simplest rhythm
signal. Natural prose sits between 0.4 and 0.8. Below 0.4 the writing
feels robotic — every sentence the same length. Above 1.0 it feels
chaotic — no discernible pattern at all. The sweet spot is variation
with intent.

alternation is the second signal. Good writing crosses the median: one
sentence above, the next below, then above again. Not perfectly — that
would be its own kind of monotony — but often enough that the reader
never settles into a drone. The alternation index measures this crossing
rate. Random text scores around 0.5. Purposeful variation scores higher.

paragraph momentum is the third. Within a paragraph, sentences can
accelerate (getting shorter — building urgency), decelerate (getting
longer — settling into reflection), or hold steady. A session where every
paragraph is steady reads like documentation. A session with variety
reads like thought.

the orthogonality matters. You can have coherent monotone prose (one
sustained argument, every sentence fifteen words). You can have varied
incoherent prose (wild sentence lengths, no logical connection between
them). Rhythm is the structural dimension that the semantic metrics
miss.

the automation implication: if a writer's prose rhythm degrades from
varied to monotone over a session arc, it is not a content problem — it
is a fatigue signal. The writer has stopped caring about how sentences
sound and is producing output to meet a quota. That is the kind of
degradation that a heartbeat monitor will never catch.
