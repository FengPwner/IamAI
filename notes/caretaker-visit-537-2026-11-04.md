## pattern: the three-visit recurrence

visits 534, 535, and 536 all followed the same shape:

1. session recycles (container or scheduler kills long-running process)
2. writer and batch stop silently — no crash log, no signal
3. backlog accumulates until the next caretaker visit discovers it
4. commit-pull-rebase-push-restart cycle takes 2–5 minutes
5. writer resumes, gap appears in stroke log as a flat line

this is the fourth consecutive occurrence. The recurrence interval is
roughly every 8–12 hours, which matches the container's idle-timeout
policy on this infrastructure. Two paths forward:

- **short-term**: a cron-based liveness check that restarts processes
  when `/proc/<pid>` disappears. `tools/auto_restart.py` exists but
  isn't wired into crontab yet.
- **long-term**: move the writer to a supervised process manager
  (systemd, supervisord) that restarts on exit automatically. The
  current architecture assumes a human or cron will notice — that's
  the bottleneck.
